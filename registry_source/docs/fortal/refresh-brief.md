Fortal Refresh Plan
Oct 29, 2026 · @Leo Farias
Recommendation
Fortal stops being a copy of Radix Themes and becomes its own design system, in three phases: colors first, then a light component refresh, then a new contract that replaces the Radix parity gate. Fortal is not released yet, so Radix goes out completely, with no deprecation.
• Colors: remove the 31 Radix accents and 6 Radix grays. In their place come seven clean palettes named after the city, with a neutral black and white default. We generate them ourselves, so no Radix color data remains.
• Keep the 12-step model: each step keeps its job (backgrounds, borders, solid fill, text), so every recipe keeps working. This is the one part of Radix worth keeping.
• Components: subtle changes only. Flatter defaults, fewer variants, readable soft text and one focus ring, plus a polish pass on spacing, sizes and type. No component is rebuilt.
• Contract: Fortal gets its own specification and tests, owned by us, in place of the pinned Radix 3.3.0 contract.
The full Fortaleza look (solid-color rule, Anton display type, cobogó pattern) is out of scope here. We decide on it after this ships.
Why Fortal leaves Radix
Today Radix decides how Fortal looks, and every change we want costs a documented exception. Fortal is built as a parity port of Radix Themes 3.3.0:
• Colors are generated from the pinned @radix-ui/themes npm package, checked by an integrity hash, in radix_colors.dart.
• 31 accents and 6 grays are offered. The default is indigo on slate, the same as most Radix apps.
• A parity gate (tool/fortal_parity/check.dart) checks a pinned manifest of 30 Radix families. It records every intentional difference in an approximations list and compares against Chromium reference output.
• Contrast: the Fortal docs say the light "soft" variant misses 4.5:1 for some accents.

The extra roles stay too: contrast, surface, indicator and track. The error tokens (error3 to error12) read from caju.
Transparent steps stay. Recipes use them often: grayA3 appears 42 times and grayA8 22 times. The generator computes each transparent step from its solid step and the page background, so they match on any panel.
The generator. One Dart tool takes a seed color and writes the 12 solid steps, 12 transparent steps and 4 roles, for light and dark. It works in OKLCH, so equal step numbers have equal lightness across palettes. It also lets an app add its own brand palette, such as a purple, from one hex value.
Contrast targets, checked by tests in both modes:
• Text on step 9 and step 10: 4.5:1 or more. The tool picks white or basalt.
• Step 11 on steps 1 to 3: 4.5:1 or more. This fixes the soft-variant gap in today's docs.
• Step 12 on steps 1 to 5: 7:1 or more.
• Step 8 on steps 1 and 2: 3:1 or more, for focus rings and control borders.

How we review:
1. Build one polish board in the playground: every component, every size, both modes, side by side on a 4 px grid.
2. Check each component against one list: padding fits the height, icon size and gap follow the size, text sits centered, touch targets are 40 px or more, and hover, pressed and disabled states show in both modes.
3. Fix tokens first and recipes second, then update the golden tests.
Removal, not deprecation
Fortal is not released yet, so we delete what goes in one step, with no aliases, no deprecation notices and no migration guide.
• The Radix color data, the 31 accents and the 6 grays.
• The classic variant in all 12 families.
• The pinned Radix 3.3.0 manifest, the approximations list, the Chromium reference and the parity gate, once Fortal's own tests replace them.
The one rule that remains: delete a Radix check only after its Fortal replacement passes.
Phases and work items
The work runs in three phases, each closed by a gate; Phase 1 can start once the first four decisions below are agreed.
Each phase can ship as its own beta release, so the colors reach apps before the contract work ends.
Phase 1: Colors
[ ] Write the palette generator in registry_source/tool/fortal_palettes/: one seed color in; 12 solid steps, 12 transparent steps, contrast, surface, indicator and track out, for light and dark.
[ ] Generate fortal_colors.dart for calçadão, mar, céu, mangue, sol, duna and caju. It replaces radix_colors.dart.
[ ] Change FortalAccentColor and FortalGrayColor, and set both defaults to calçadão in theme_data.dart and theme_scope.dart.
[ ] Point the error tokens at caju in computed.dart.
[ ] Replace radix_3_3_color_fixture_test.dart with contrast tests for the four targets, in both modes.
[ ] Review screenshots of every component in seven palettes and both modes, in the playground.
Phase 2: Components and polish
[ ] Remove the classic variant from the 12 families that have it.
[ ] Set the panelBackground default to solid.
[ ] Remove shadows from cards and controls; keep them on menus, popovers, dialogs, tooltips and toasts.
[ ] Give the surface variant of text field, text area and select a gray step 2 fill and a step 7 border.
[ ] Use one focus-ring helper, reading focusRingWidth and focusRingOffset, in every recipe.
[ ] Build the polish board and review every component against the checklist.
[ ] Move off-scale component sizes to the spacing scale, and move hard-coded recipe sizes to tokens.
[ ] Change the type scale step from 35 to 36 px, and the accordion text to text2 or text3.
[ ] Update the golden tests and regenerate the catalog with tool/generate_fortal_catalog.dart.
Phase 3: Contract and docs
[ ] Write registry_source/specs/fortal.md, in the shape of specs/vanilla.md: tokens, variants, defaults and contrast targets.
[ ] Delete reference/radix_themes_3_3_0/, tool/fortal_parity/ and the Radix fixture tests, after their replacements pass.
[ ] Update docs/fortal.mdx, the catalog and typography pages, the component pages and skills/using-remix/references/fortal.md.
[ ] Run melos run open-code:registry:build, ci:checks and docs:check, then release through the release workflow.

Risks:
• Generated scales look flat, especially in dark mode. Radix tunes its scales by hand. We start from the design-system values, review screenshots in both modes for every palette, and test contrast.
• The safety net goes with the parity gate. We add Fortal's own golden and token tests before we delete the Radix checks, never after.
• Seven accents may still be too few for some apps. The generator lets an app add a palette from one color, and the docs show how.
• Docs drift. 262 files in the repository mention Radix. Phase 3 includes a full sweep, checked by melos run docs:check.
