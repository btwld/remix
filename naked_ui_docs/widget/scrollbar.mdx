description: State-aware scrollbar over Flutter RawScrollbar
keywords: [flutter, headless, scrollbar]
---

Pass the same `ScrollController` to `NakedScrollbar` and its scrollable. The wrapper retains `RawScrollbar` painting, fading, hit testing and thumb dragging. `thumbColor`, `trackColor`, `trackBorderColor` and `thickness` accept `WidgetStateProperty` values for hover and drag styling.

`thumbVisibility`, `trackVisibility`, `interactive`, `radius`, margins and fade durations control presentation. A visible track requires a visible thumb. Do not combine `shape` and `radius`; dispose controllers owned by your widget.
