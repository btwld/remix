05Separate checks. Visible limits.

NO BLANKET QUALITY SCORE
TECHNICAL

Repeatable validation

Matrix, schemas, path safety, baseline hashes, geometry, exports, and rebuild checks. Current results are in the package reports.

OPTICAL

Local review evidence

Native-size drawings and critical gaps inspected. Original artwork preserved. Independent design approval is still pending.

INTERFACE

Real browser checks

Test scopes include URL/file loading, layout, downloads, focus, and toggles. A test being present is not a pass; actual browser results and limits are recorded in reports.

RECOGNITION

Not user-tested

No recognition score is claimed. Warning, Branch, Focus, and Help need labeled context in dense interfaces.

Shared grammar, not identical bounding boxes

Square terminals, deliberate diagonals, and nominal 1 / 1.5 / 2 px outlines. Dense icons can occupy smaller fields. Open symbols use regular/heavier pairs.

Size is part of the design

Use the 12 px master up to 12 px, the 16 px master at 14–16 px, and the 24 px master from 20 px. Below 12 px is not reviewed. Intermediate sizes can soften pixel alignment.

One identity for each concept

Sliders adjusts parameters. Filter narrows results. Gear opens settings. “Settings” is a search keyword, not a renamed Sliders API.

A component owns its accessible name

Hide decorative inner SVGs from assistive technology and label the button. Selected state changes aria-pressed without changing the action name. Color alone is not the meaning.

Known exception carried forwardWarning’s outline mark-to-frame gaps are approximately 0.50 / 1.03 / 1.58 px at 12 / 16 / 24 px. These miss the general targets of 1 / 1.5 / 2 px. The artwork is preserved. Visible text label required for outline at 12 / 16 / 24 px and filled at 12 / 16 / 24 px. This also applies to scaled uses of those masters. A tooltip or accessible name alone does not replace the visible label. Filled 16/24 px negative punctuation also has a documented below-target width. Named ownership and independent approval remain pending.

Decision for every icon

Home — preserved baseline

Home or top-level destination. Do not substitute for Back.

Retained the roof, doorway, and body pair. The silhouette remains a useful family reference without requiring all icons to match its area. At 16 px, the roof/body has a small documented overshoot beyond the nominal 1.5 px padding.

Use the action name on the enclosing control; 12 px is for labeled information.

Pair model: silhouette. Independent design approval: pending. Recognition: not user-tested.

Menu — preserved baseline

Open navigation or a menu.

Retained the three bars after comparing crisp and strictly centered micro variants. The original 12 px outline raster is clearer. The 0.5 px optical offset is intentional. A larger clickable area and an accessible name are still required.

Use the action name on the enclosing control; 12 px is for labeled information.

Pair model: weight. Independent design approval: pending. Recognition: not user-tested.

More — preserved baseline

Additional actions in an overflow menu.

Retained crisp 1 px micro dots rather than replacing them with blurred centered marks. The 12 px offset is intentional and documented; larger masters remain balanced. Use 16 px or larger for an overflow control, with an accessible More options name.

Use the action name on the enclosing control; 12 px is for labeled information.

Pair model: weight. Independent design approval: pending. Recognition: not user-tested.

Search — preserved baseline

Find content. A heavier lens still has an open center.

Retained the open octagonal lens and diagonal handle. The lens stays transparent in both weights; filling it would change the metaphor. The 12 px filled lens is relatively dense; use a label or a clearly identified search field.

Use the action name on the enclosing control; 12 px is for labeled information.

Pair model: weight. Independent design approval: pending. Recognition: not user-tested.

Grid — preserved baseline

Open an application grid or change to grid view.

Reduced the 24 px footprint and increased separation at 16 px. Four frames no longer dominate as strongly. Native opening widths are 2 / 2 / 3 px. Four repeated cells naturally paint more area than one arrow. Equal area is not a target.

Use the action name on the enclosing control; 12 px is for labeled information.

Pair model: silhouette. Independent design approval: pending. Recognition: not user-tested.
