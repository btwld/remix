Spacing / construction exceptions

  • warning-gap-16 — warning outline: Chamfered caution plate cannot host 1/1.5/2 px punctuation with full side and base clearance at this native size. Visible-label restriction remains concept-level and is not weakened.
  • warning-gap-24 — warning outline: Chamfered caution plate cannot host 1/1.5/2 px punctuation with full side and base clearance at this native size. Visible-label restriction remains concept-level and is not weakened.
  • branch-chamfer-12 — branch master 12: Full chamfer token would collapse the outline ring on this dense plate (knob or junction box). Construction uses the reduced cut; styles.json lists the same override.

Open questions for human review

Construction does not decide these. They remain named questions for a short recognition pass.

  • search-as-viewfinder (search) — Is a chamfered-square viewfinder still recognizable as Search next to crop/frame/view tools?
  • cad-star-as-favorite (star) — Does a regular (not optically shifted) five-point star read as Favorite, or as a CAD/badge mark?
  • plus-keyline-mixed-toolbar (plus) — Should Technical plus keep its tighter inset, or share Angular's outer keyline in mixed-pack toolbars?
  • density-12-link-branch (link, branch) — Are 12 px Link shackles and Branch junction boxes recognizable at native size, or must they stay labeled-only?
