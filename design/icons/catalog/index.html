A tooltip or accessible name alone does not replace the visible label. The rule applies to Outline and Rounded. A recorded gap exception does not lift it.

Grammar

Each family is constructed from its own primitives. Comparison on this page reads finished SVGs; it does not refit one family onto another.

Outline

Validated geometry at native 12 / 16 / 24. Corners: sharp or deliberate chamfer. Terminals: square. Outline and filled path commands are M L Z.

Rounded

Rounded icons are constructed from Rounded primitives, not offset Outline paths. object outer corners use CORNER_TOKEN (~1.6–1.7× outline); acute cues use ACUTE_JOIN (~1.25–1.33× outline), on the keyline; boolean cut terminals use CUT_JOIN (0.3× outline); star uses STAR_JOIN (~0.5×) so five-point mass survives. Terminals are round. Product commands are M L Z. The undrawn fillet proposal previously called Rounded (0.5–1× on Outline) needs a product naming decision.
